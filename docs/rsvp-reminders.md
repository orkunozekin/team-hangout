# RSVP reminders and delivery diagnostics

P2.15 adds a server-owned reminder for unanswered or needs-reconfirmation participants, plus a staff diagnostics page that keeps provider failure separate from human acknowledgement.

Reminders are not phone timers. Each run prepares at most 25 soonest published events by start time and at most 25 published events whose RSVP deadline falls inside the next 25 hours, then dispatches at most 25 due reminder jobs. A deadline inside that window is prepared even when the event start is outside the 16-week start scan, including a reminder that was paused while the flag was off. The default is one reminder 24 hours before the RSVP deadline. If the event or deadline appears with at least one hour and less than 24 hours remaining, the worker sends one immediate reminder. Nothing is sent during the final hour, after the deadline, or while the event is unpublished, cancelled, postponed, completed, or on a closed resource.

The reminder identity is the schedule version plus the deadline instant, stored on the outbox intent as `reminderKey`. Moving the deadline or rescheduling the event plans a new reminder. An already sent reminder is not recalled and does not count as the reminder for the new version. Repeated scheduler runs reuse the deterministic outbox id. Expansion of a still-pending `rsvpReminder` intent sends nothing unless the organization flag is on, the event is still published and scheduled, the resource is open, the stored key still matches, and at least one hour remains.

Recipients are deduplicated login accounts that can respond: the linked participant, or a guardian whose current grant includes `respond`. Yes, no, and maybe are responses. View-only guardians, coaches, and staff do not receive the reminder. Expansion checks the live roster and response again, so a response or revoked grant drops that recipient before inbox creation. The organization flag `features.rsvpReminders` must be true. Inbox, push, and email still follow their own flags.

Staff open Delivery diagnostics in the admin workspace. Delivery failures are permanent or unknown transport results, including `EMAIL_BOUNCED` and `DeviceNotRegistered`. Unacknowledged critical updates are a separate list. Each list pages 25 rows, and Show more appends the next page from that list's cursor. Reading an in-app notification does not acknowledge it, and a bounce does not either. The page does not show email addresses, push tokens, or recipient ids. Program staff see only their programs. Client reads and writes of reminder jobs, delivery issues, and critical projections stay denied. Listing the inbox deletes a critical projection when that recipient has lost access, including a critical update that never had a push or email delivery. A revoked row kept only as a page cursor is left in place so the cursor stays valid.

Account deletion removes that person's delivery-issue and critical-update projections. Rollback is disabling `features.rsvpReminders`. Existing reminder history and idempotency records stay in place. Deploy the reminder and critical-update indexes before enabling the flag. This change does not enable the flag, send live email, or prove device delivery.
